## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Visual Style
- **Color Palette:** Dark navy blue (#0A1929), electric blue (#00D4FF), white
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif (Inter or Circular), bold headlines
- **Animation Style:** Clean, professional motion graphics - not cheesy
- **Screen Recordings:** Real interface, not mockups - builds credibility

### Voiceover Direction
- **Gender:** Neutral (male or female works)
- **Age:** 30-45 sound (credible but not stuffy)
- **Tone:** Confident consultant, not infomercial salesperson
- **Pace:** Moderate - not rushed, space for visuals to breathe
- **Energy:** Problem sections = frustrated/sympathetic, Solution sections = energetic/optimistic

### Music
- **Track 1 (0:00-0:35):** Subtle tension, problem-focused
- **Track 2 (0:35-3:00):** Upbeat, modern, tech-forward (think Stripe/Notion style)
- **Volume:** Under narration, swells during visual-only moments

### On-Screen Text
- **Use sparingly:** Only for key data points and CTAs
- **Animation:** Slide in from left, fade out - consistent throughout
- **Duration:** 2-3 seconds minimum for readability

### Key Frames to Emphasize
- 0:05 - The "bad ChatGPT output" (relatable pain)
- 0:40 - The three-stage diagram (core concept)
- 1:20 - Competitive analysis in action (unique value)
- 2:15 - Comparison table (differentiation)
- 2:50 - CTA buttons (conversion moment)
