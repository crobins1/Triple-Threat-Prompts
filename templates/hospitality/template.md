## Content Themes for Hospitality

**High-Conversion Content:**

1. **Room tours with "imagine yourself here"** - Showcase rooms during golden hour, emphasize experience not just features
2. **Guest testimonials** - Video of real guests sharing their experience
3. **"Best things to do in [location]" guides** - Position as local expert
4. **Seasonal property beauty** - Fall colors, spring blooms, winter coziness, summer vibes
5. **Behind-the-scenes** - Staff preparing rooms, chef creating breakfast, day-in-the-life
6. **Special moments** - Proposals, anniversaries, guest celebrations (with permission)
7. **Amenity highlights** - Rooftop sunset, pool day, spa treatment, breakfast spread
8. **Local partnerships** - Feature local restaurants, tour companies, experiences you recommend
9. **Pet-friendly content** (if applicable) - Guest dogs enjoying the property
10. **"Day in the life staying at [Property]"** - Full experience walkthrough

### Content Framework: "Experiential Storytelling"

Hospitality content sells the experience, not just the accommodation:

- **50% aspirational imagery** - Beautiful property and location visuals
- **30% guest experience** - Real guests, testimonials, user-generated content
- **20% practical information** - Amenities, location, booking offers

**Why this works:**
- People book hotels based on how they imagine feeling there
- User-generated content provides social proof
- Showcasing location adds value beyond the room
- Direct bookings are driven by trust and experience promise

## Platform-Specific Strategy for Hotels

### Instagram Strategy (Visual Appeal + Booking Intent)

**Goal:** Showcase property beauty, build desire, drive direct bookings

**Feed Strategy:**
- Post 4-6x per week
- Carousel posts work well (room features across multiple images)
- Golden hour photography (sunrise/sunset always performs well)

## Unique Challenges for Hospitality Content

### Challenge 1: OTA Dependency (Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb)

**Problem:** OTAs charge 15-25% commission on bookings

**Solution:**
- **Direct booking incentives**: "Book direct and save 15%" (pass through commission savings)
- **Exclusive perks for direct bookings**: "Free breakfast when you book direct"
- **Content drives to website, not OTAs**: All social links go to property website
- **Email capture**: Build email list, nurture with offers
- **Retargeting ads**: Remarket to website visitors with direct booking offer

### Challenge 2: Seasonal Occupancy Fluctuations

**Problem:** 85% occupancy in peak, 50% in off-season

**Solution:**
- **Off-season content**: "5 reasons to visit [location] in winter"
- **Seasonal packages**: "Winter wellness retreat package - 3 nights + spa"
- **Local event tie-ins**: "Stay with us for [local festival]"
- **Staycation targeting**: Market to locals for off-season weekends
- **Long-term stay promotions**: "Work remotely from the beach - monthly rates"

### Challenge 3: Standing Out from Chain Hotels

**Problem:** Chains have brand recognition and loyalty programs

**Solution:**
- **Emphasize unique character**: "No two rooms are the same" vs. cookie-cutter
- **Local experience positioning**: "Owned by locals, experience Santa Barbara like a local"
- **Personalization**: "We remember your coffee order" vs. corporate
- **Sustainability and values**: Appeal to conscious travelers
- **Authentic storytelling**: Show real staff, real guests, real experiences

### Challenge 4: User-Generated Content Permission

**Problem:** Guests post great content, but you need permission to use it

**Solution:**
- **Check-in card**: "Tag us @coastalhaven for a chance to be featured!"
- **Incentivize**: "Share your stay and get 10% off your next visit"
- **Always ask permission**: DM before reposting
- **Create photo opportunities**: Instagrammable spots around property with signage
- **Branded hashtag**: #MyCoastalHaven or similar

## Content Production Workflow for Hotels

### Time Investment: 4-6 hours/week

**Option 1: In-House Marketing Manager**
- **Daily (20 min):** Capture property moments (sunrise, guest experiences, amenities)
- **Weekly (2 hours):** Plan and schedule week's content
- **Monthly (4 hours):** Produce one long-form video (property tour, area guide)
- **Ongoing:** Respond to comments and DMs, engage with guests who tag property

**Option 2: Front Desk/Concierge as Content Creator**
- Designate one team member to capture daily content
- Give them 30 min per shift for content creation
- Pay bonus ($200-500/month) for social media duties
- They're naturally positioned to interact with happy guests

**Option 3: Using Triple-Threat System**
- **Input:** Property details, location, current promotions, guest testimonials
- **Output:** Optimized captions, content calendar, direct booking CTAs, area guides
- **Still need:** Property photography and videography (hire photographer quarterly)
- **Time saved:** 60-80% on strategy and copywriting
