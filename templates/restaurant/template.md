## Restaurant Content Strategy

### Visual-First Food Content

**Food Photography (40%)**
- Hero dish glamour shots
- Behind-the-scenes prep
- Plating process videos
- Ingredient sourcing

**Customer Experience (25%)**
- Atmosphere and ambiance
- Table settings and decor
- Happy diners (with permission)
- Special events and celebrations

**Staff & Culture (20%)**
- Chef and kitchen team
- Server personalities
- Cooking process
- Team member spotlights

**Community Engagement (15%)**
- Local partnerships
- Events and specials
- Seasonal menus
- Customer features (regulars, anniversaries)

### Platform Strategies

**Instagram (PRIMARY for Restaurants)**
- Feed: Beautiful food photography, ambiance shots
- Reels: Cooking process, dish assembly, day-in-the-life
- Stories: Daily specials, wait times, menu updates, polls

**Facebook**
- Menu updates and specials
- Events and private dining
- Community engagement
- Review response

**TikTok**
- Behind-the-scenes kitchen
- Chef personality content
- Satisfying cooking videos
- Food trends and challenges

**Google My Business (CRITICAL)**
- Menu updates
- Photos
- Review responses
- Special hours
