## 🎨 Brand Consistency Guide

### Visual Style
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary: #0A1929 (Navy)
  - Accent: #00D4FF (Electric Blue)
  - Background: #FFFFFF (White)
  - Text: #1A1A1A (Near Black)

- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: Inter Bold or Circular Bold
  - Body: Inter Regular or Circular Book
  - Code/Technical: JetBrains Mono

- **Animation Style:**
  - Smooth, professional motion
  - 0.3-0.5 second transitions
  - Subtle easing (not bouncy)
  - Modern tech aesthetic (think Stripe, Notion, Linear)

### Voiceover Style
- **Tone:** Confident consultant, not salesperson
- **Pace:** Moderate (140-160 words/minute)
- **Age:** 30-45 sound (credible but not stuffy)
- **Gender:** Neutral (either works)
- **Accent:** Clear, minimal regional accent

### Music
- **Genre:** Modern electronic, ambient tech, corporate uplift
- **Volume:** -20 to -25 dB under voiceover
- **Sources:** Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed
- **Avoid:** Stock ukulele, generic corporate piano

## 📱 Platform-Specific Optimization

### YouTube
- **Format:** 16:9 horizontal
- **Length:** 3-8 minutes ideal
- **Title:** Keyword-rich ("AI Content Strategy System for [Industry]")
- **Thumbnail:** High contrast, text overlay, faces if possible
- **Description:** First 150 characters matter most
- **Cards/End Screens:** Link to website, related videos

### Website/Landing Page
- **Format:** 16:9 or 1:1 (mobile)
- **Length:** 60-90 seconds max
- **Hosting:** Wistia (best for conversion tracking) or Vimeo
- **Autoplay:** Yes, but muted with captions
- **Loading:** Optimize file size <10MB

### TikTok/Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical
- **Length:** 15-60 seconds
- **Captions:** Always ON (80% watch muted)
- **Hook:** First 3 seconds critical
- **Text Overlays:** Large, readable on mobile

## 🔧 Technical Specifications

### Video Export Settings

**For Web (Homepage, Landing Pages):**
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- Frame Rate: 30fps
- Codec: H.264
- Bitrate: 5-8 Mbps
- Audio: 128 kbps AAC
- Container: MP4

**For Social Media:**
- Resolution: 1080x1920 (vertical) or 1920x1080 (horizontal)
- Frame Rate: 30fps (TikTok/Reels) or 60fps (optional for smooth motion)
- Codec: H.264
- Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps
- Audio: 192 kbps AAC

**For YouTube:**
- Resolution: 1920x1080 minimum (4K if possible)
- Frame Rate: 24fps, 30fps, or 60fps
- Codec: H.264 or H.265
- Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps (1080p) or 35-45 Mbps (4K)
- Audio: 192-320 kbps AAC

### Subtitle/Caption Files
- **Format:** SRT or VTT
- **Font:** Sans-serif, high contrast
- **Size:** 32-40pt for web, larger for mobile
- **Position:** Lower third, centered
- **Color:** White text with black outline/shadow

## 📈 Testing & Optimization

**Test First:**
1. Hook (first 3-5 seconds) - biggest impact

3. Length (shorter vs. longer)

4. Voiceover (male vs. female)

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Better Engagement
- **Pattern Interrupt:** Start with unexpected visual or statement
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Screen recordings > talking head describing
- **Social Proof Early:** Testimonials in first 30 seconds boost trust
- **Captions Always:** 80%+ watch social media videos muted

### For Better Conversion
- **One CTA:** Don't give multiple options (decision paralysis)
- **Create Urgency:** Limited time offers, scarcity messaging
- **Address Objections:** Tackle price, complexity, time investment head-on
- **Guarantee/Risk Reversal:** Money-back guarantee reduces friction

### For Production Efficiency
- **Batch Record:** Do all voiceovers in one session
- **Template Everything:** Create reusable intro/outro templates
- **Stock Library:** Build library of b-roll, screenshots, animations
- **Repurpose:** One 3-min video = 10 short clips for social
