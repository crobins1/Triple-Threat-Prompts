## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Platform Optimization

**TikTok:**
- Vertical 9:16 format
- Captions ON (80% watch without sound)
- Fast cuts (3-5 sec per shot max)
- Trending audio optional (use for discovery, not if teaching)
- On-screen text in TikTok native font style

**Instagram Reels:**
- Vertical 9:16 format
- Aesthetic matters more than TikTok
- Slower pace acceptable
- Brand colors and fonts
- Strong first frame (people scroll past if boring)

**YouTube Shorts:**
- Vertical 9:16 format
- Can be slightly longer (up to 60 sec)
- More tolerant of educational content
- SEO title matters ("AI Content Strategy for [Industry]")

### Creator Presence
- **Option A:** Talking head throughout (builds personal brand)
- **Option B:** Screen recording only (faster to produce)
- **Option C:** Hybrid (hook on camera, demo via screen, CTA back to camera)

### Hook Psychology
- **Pattern Interrupt:** Unexpected statement or visual
- **Pain Point:** "Are you struggling with..."
- **Time/Money:** "Stop spending X hours on Y..."
- **Curiosity Gap:** "This changed everything..."
- **Direct Call-Out:** "If you're a [profession]..."

### Testing Strategy
1. Post Script 1, 2, 3 in week 1
2. Measure view-through rate and profile visits
3. Double down on highest performer
4. Create 3 variations of winner
5. Repeat

## QUICK PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

1. **Script Selection:** Choose 1-2 scripts per day
2. **Film Setup:** Ring light, phone on tripod, clean background
3. **Record:** 3-5 takes of each section (choose best in edit)
4. **Screen Recordings:** Record demos in advance, keep library
5. **Edit:** CapCut (free) or Descript ($20/mo)
6. **Captions:** Auto-generate, clean up typos
7. **Post:** Schedule across all 3 platforms
8. **Engage:** Reply to comments within first hour

**Time Investment:** 2-3 hours to batch-create 5 videos
**Post Schedule:** 1/day across TikTok, Reels, Shorts
**Results Timeline:** 30 days to see patterns, 90 days to optimize
