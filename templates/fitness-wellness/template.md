## Fitness Content Strategy

### Content That Converts for Fitness

**Transformation Content (35%)**
- Before/after photos (with permission)
- Client success story features
- Progress check-ins and milestones
- Testimonial videos

**Educational Workouts (30%)**
- Exercise demonstrations
- Quick home workouts
- Form tips and corrections
- Workout challenges

**Motivation & Lifestyle (20%)**
- Inspirational quotes with context
- Behind-the-scenes training
- Nutrition tips (within scope of practice)
- Recovery and self-care

**Community Building (15%)**
- Member spotlights
- Class energy/culture showcase
- Challenges and competitions
- Celebration of milestones

### Platform Strategies

**Instagram (PRIMARY)**
- Reels: 15-30sec exercise demos, transformation reveals, workout snippets
- Feed: Transformation photos, educational carousels, community celebration
- Stories: Daily class energy, form tips, member shoutouts, Q&A

**TikTok**
- Quick workout demos
- Fitness myths debunked
- Day in the life of trainer
- Beginner-friendly content

**YouTube**
- Full workout videos (lead magnet for paid programs)
- Exercise library with proper form
- Client transformation interviews
- Nutrition and recovery content

**Facebook**
- Local community engagement
- Class schedule updates
- Challenge groups (30-day challenges)
- Member community discussions
