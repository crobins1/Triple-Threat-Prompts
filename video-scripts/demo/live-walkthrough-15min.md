# Live Demo Walkthrough (15 Minutes)

**Purpose:** Sales calls, demo requests, onboarding calls
**Platform:** Zoom screen share, Loom, product demo
**Audience:** Qualified prospects who've requested a demo
**Goal:** Convert demo attendee to customer

---

## DEMO SCRIPT

- Have n8n workflow open and ready
- Prepare example business (use prospect's industry if known)
- Have 2-3 sample outputs ready to show if system is slow

- Clear browser history/bookmarks (looks professional)

## OBJECTION HANDLING

### "Can't I just use ChatGPT?"

**YOU:**
"You absolutely can. And you'll get generic content that sounds like everyone else's. Here's the difference..."

**[Show side-by-side: ChatGPT output vs. Triple-Threat output]**

**YOU:**
"ChatGPT gives you content. This gives you STRATEGY. ChatGPT doesn't analyze your competitors. Doesn't optimize for platforms. Doesn't diagnose performance. It's like comparing a typewriter to a strategic consultant."

### "This seems expensive"

**YOU:**
"I get it. Let me ask - how much time do you spend on content per week?"

**[They answer: usually 5-10 hours]**

**YOU:**
"And what's your time worth? If you're billing at $[their rate] per hour, you're spending $[calculated] per week on content. That's $[annual] per year. This system gets you to 90 minutes per week - it pays for itself in [timeframe]."

**Alternative approach:**
"What's a qualified lead worth to your business? $500? $1,000? If this generates 3 extra leads in the first month from better content, it's paid for itself."

### "I need to think about it"

**YOU:**
"Totally fair. What specifically do you need to think about? Is it the price, the features, timing, or something else?"

**[Let them answer specifically]**

**YOU:**
"I ask because [address their specific concern]. Would it help if I [offer specific solution]?"

**Options:**
- "Would a payment plan make more sense?"
- "Want me to run a quick analysis of your competitors right now so you can see what gaps exist?"
- "Should we schedule a follow-up after you [specific next step]?"

### "We're already working with [competitor]"

**YOU:**
"What's working well with them?"

**[Listen]**

**YOU:**
"And what's not working as well as you'd like?"

**[This reveals their pain - then address it specifically]**

**YOU:**
"Got it. Here's how we're different: [specific differentiation based on their pain]."

## DEMO BEST PRACTICES

### Pre-Call Preparation
- ✅ Review prospect's LinkedIn/website beforehand
- ✅ Identify 3-5 competitors in advance
- ✅ Have industry template ready to show
- ✅ Prepare 2-3 examples in their niche
- ✅ Test screen share and audio

### During Demo
- ✅ Speak at 80% speed (people process slower over video)
- ✅ Pause after key points for questions
- ✅ Ask confirming questions ("Make sense so far?")
- ✅ Use their specific pain points in examples
- ✅ Let THEM talk 40% of the time

### After Demo
- ✅ Send follow-up email within 1 hour
- ✅ Include recording link (if permitted)
- ✅ Attach relevant case study or template preview
- ✅ Clear next steps
- ✅ Set follow-up calendar hold

## DEMO RECORDING TIPS

### If Recording for Async Use
- **Length:** Cut to 8-10 minutes (attention span)
- **Intro:** Skip pleasantries, get straight to value
- **Pacing:** Faster than live demo
- **Captions:** Must have for async watching
- **CTA:** Clear next step at end

### Recording Setup
- **Screen:** 1920x1080 minimum
- **Audio:** USB mic or quality headset
- **Background:** Blur or professional
- **Notifications:** Turn OFF everything
- **Cursor:** Use cursor highlighting software

## SUCCESS METRICS

**Demo Quality Indicators:**
- Prospect asks 3+ questions (engagement)
- Prospect references their specific use case (personalization working)
- Demo completion rate >85% (not boring)
- Calendar hold for follow-up (buying intent)

**Conversion Benchmarks:**
- Cold demo (from website): 15-25% close rate
- Warm demo (referral/content): 35-50% close rate
- Hot demo (enterprise inquiry): 60-75% close rate

**If below benchmarks, check:**
- Are you showing value in first 3 minutes?
- Are you personalizing to their business?
- Are you handling objections confidently?
- Is your close clear and direct?
