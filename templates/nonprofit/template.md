# Nonprofit & Charity Content Template

## Industry: Nonprofits, Charities, Foundations, Social Enterprises

For nonprofit organizations building awareness, engaging donors, recruiting volunteers, and driving fundraising through strategic content.

## Template Variables

```json
{
  "ORGANIZATION_NAME": "Your nonprofit name",
  "MISSION_FOCUS": "Cause area (e.g., 'homeless services', 'environmental conservation', 'education access', 'animal rescue')",
  "GEOGRAPHIC_SCOPE": "Local, regional, national, international",
  "TARGET_DONORS": "Individual donors, corporate sponsors, foundations, major gifts",
  "DONOR_DEMOGRAPHICS": "Age, income level, values, motivations",
  "PROGRAMS_SERVICES": "What you actually do (direct service, advocacy, research, grants)",
  "IMPACT_METRICS": "Lives touched, dollars distributed, measurable outcomes",
  "FUNDRAISING_MODEL": "Individual giving, events, grants, corporate partnerships, earned revenue",
  "VOLUNTEER_NEEDS": "Types of volunteers needed and roles",
  "DONATION_LEVELS": "Giving tiers and what they fund",
  "TRANSPARENCY": "Charity rating (Charity Navigator, GuideStar), overhead percentage",
  "EMOTIONAL_HOOK": "The human story that connects people to your cause",
  "COMPETITOR_NONPROFITS": "3-5 similar organizations",
  "BUSINESS_GOAL": "Donor acquisition, recurring giving, volunteer recruitment, awareness, advocacy"
}
```

## Nonprofit Content Strategy

### Content Pillars

**Impact Stories (45%)**
- Beneficiary success stories
- Before/after transformations
- Day-in-the-life of those served
- Volunteer impact stories
- Donor impact demonstrations

**Educational/Awareness (25%)**
- Issue education (why this cause matters)
- Statistics and research
- Advocacy and policy updates
- Myth-busting
- Community resources

**Behind-the-Scenes (20%)**
- Staff and volunteer spotlights
- Programs in action
- Donor dollars at work
- Organizational milestones
- Transparency reports

**Calls-to-Action (10%)**
- Donation campaigns
- Volunteer opportunities
- Event promotions
- Petition/advocacy actions
- Monthly giving programs

### Platform Strategies

**Facebook (PRIMARY for Many Nonprofits)**
- Impact stories with emotion
- Fundraising campaigns
- Event promotion
- Volunteer recruitment
- Community building

**Instagram**
- Visual storytelling
- Beneficiary features
- Volunteer highlights
- Behind-the-scenes
- Stories for daily engagement

**Email (CRITICAL for Donor Retention)**
- Monthly impact reports
- Donor appreciation
- Campaign updates
- Recurring giving asks
- Year-end appeals

**LinkedIn (Corporate Partnerships)**
- CSR partnership opportunities
- Corporate volunteer programs
- Thought leadership on cause
- Board member recruitment
