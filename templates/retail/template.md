## Retail Content Strategy

### Content Pillars

**Product Showcases (40%)**
- New arrivals and restocks
- Product styling and use cases
- "Just in" posts
- Gift guides and collections
- Product details and quality highlights
- Behind-the-product stories

**Community & Local Connection (30%)**
- Customer features and testimonials
- Local partnerships and collaborations
- Events and workshops
- Community involvement
- Supporting local causes
- Shop local advocacy

**Shop Experience (20%)**
- Store ambiance and displays
- Behind-the-scenes
- Owner/staff introductions
- Day in the life
- Window displays and merchandising
- "Come visit us" invitations

**Promotions & Events (10%)**
- Sales and special offers
- In-store events
- Gift with purchase
- Loyalty rewards
- Seasonal promotions
- Last-chance and limited items

### Platform Strategies

**Instagram (PRIMARY for Retail)**
- Product photos and styling
- Stories for daily engagement
- Reels showcasing products
- Community reposts
- Behind-the-scenes
- Local hashtags

**Facebook (Local Community)**
- Event announcements
- Community engagement
- Local group participation
- Longer storytelling
- Customer testimonials
- Special promotions

**Google Business Profile (Local Discovery)**
- Complete business information
- Regular photo updates
- Respond to all reviews
- Posts about new products/events
- Local SEO critical

**TikTok (Product Discovery + Younger Demos)**
- Product unboxing and reveals
- "Shop with me" videos
- Owner personality and story
- Local business advocacy
- Trending formats with products
