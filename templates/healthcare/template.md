## Healthcare Content Strategy Framework

### Key Compliance Considerations
⚠️ **HIPAA Compliance:** All content must avoid patient identifiable information without explicit written consent
⚠️ **Medical Claims:** Cannot make guarantees or unverifiable treatment claims
⚠️ **Professional Standards:** Must adhere to state medical board guidelines

### Content Pillars for Healthcare

**1. Patient Education (40% of content)**
- Condition explanations in plain language
- Prevention tips and wellness advice
- Treatment option comparisons
- Recovery/aftercare guidance

**2. Practice Information (25%)**
- Meet the team/provider background
- Office tour and technology
- What to expect at appointments
- Insurance and payment options

**3. Community Engagement (20%)**
- Local health initiatives
- Seasonal health tips (flu season, summer safety)
- Health awareness months
- Practice involvement in community

**4. Social Proof (15%)**
- Patient testimonials (HIPAA-compliant)
- Before/after (with consent, especially for cosmetic/dental)
- Awards, certifications, continuing education
- Professional affiliations
