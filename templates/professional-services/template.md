# Professional Services Content Template

## Industry: Consultants, Accountants, Attorneys, Architects, Engineers

For knowledge-based professional services firms that need to demonstrate expertise and build trust with corporate or individual clients.

## Professional Services Content Strategy

### Thought Leadership Framework

**Expertise Demonstration (50%)**
- Industry trend analysis
- Regulatory/legislative updates
- Case studies (anonymized if needed)
- Best practices and frameworks
- Common mistakes and how to avoid

**Firm Positioning (30%)**
- Methodology explanations
- Team expertise spotlights
- Awards and recognition
- Client testimonials
- Published work and speaking engagements

**Business Development (20%)**
- Service line education
- When to hire a [professional]
- ROI of services
- Initial consultation offers

### Platform Strategies

**LinkedIn (PRIMARY for B2B Services)**
- Weekly thought leadership articles
- Industry commentary
- Client success stories
- Team expertise highlights
- Professional development

**Industry Publications**
- Guest articles in trade magazines
- Whitepapers and research
- Conference speaking

**Email Newsletter**
- Monthly insights for client base
- Industry updates affecting their business
- Firm news and capabilities

**Website Blog + SEO**
- Comprehensive guides on key topics
- FAQ content
- Local SEO for regional firms
