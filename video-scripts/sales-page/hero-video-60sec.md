## PRODUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

### Technical Requirements
- **Format:** 16:9 (desktop) or 1:1 (mobile-optimized version)
- **Resolution:** Minimum 1080p
- **File Size:** Under 10MB for fast page load
- **Encoding:** H.264, MP4 container
- **Bitrate:** 5-8 Mbps video, 128 kbps audio

### Autoplay Settings
- **Default:** Muted with subtitles ON
- **Loop:** No - play once, then show poster frame
- **Mobile:** Respect data-saver settings
- **Fallback:** Animated GIF or static image if video doesn't load

### Subtitle Requirements
- **Font:** Sans-serif, 32-40pt
- **Color:** White with black outline/shadow
- **Position:** Lower third, centered
- **Timing:** Sync precisely with narration
- **Style:** Max 2 lines, 40 characters per line

### Visual Brand Guidelines
- **Primary Color:** #0A1929 (Navy)
- **Accent Color:** #00D4FF (Electric Blue)
- **Typography:** Inter or Circular (modern sans-serif)
- **Animation:** Smooth, professional - no bouncy/cartoony effects
- **Transitions:** Cross-dissolve, wipe, fade - subtle

### Captions
- **Required:** Full transcript in subtitles
- **Timing:** Accurate sync with audio

- **Optional but recommended:** Describe key visuals for screen readers
- **Implementation:** Separate audio track with descriptions

### Keyboard Navigation
- **Play/Pause:** Spacebar
- **Mute/Unmute:** M key
