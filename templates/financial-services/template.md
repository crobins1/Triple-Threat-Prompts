## Content Strategy (Compliance-First)

### Regulatory Compliance Requirements
⚠️ **No Guarantees:** Cannot promise returns or outcomes
⚠️ **Testimonial Restrictions:** Many states prohibit client testimonials
⚠️ **Disclosures:** Must include appropriate disclaimers
⚠️ **Archiving:** May need to archive all communications
⚠️ **Supervision:** Content may require compliance review before posting

### Content Pillars

**Educational (60%)** - Safe, valuable, positions as expert
- Retirement planning strategies
- Tax-efficient investing concepts
- Estate planning basics
- Market commentary (without predictions)
- Financial planning milestones by age/life stage

**Thought Leadership (25%)** - Build authority
- Economic trends analysis
- Legislative changes impact (tax law, retirement rules)
- Common planning mistakes
- Industry myth-busting

**Practice Information (15%)** - Build trust
- Your planning philosophy
- Client experience overview
- Team member spotlights
- Community involvement

### Platform Strategies

**LinkedIn (PRIMARY for Financial Services)**
- Weekly market commentary (educational, not predictive)
- Financial planning tips
- Client success stories (without identifying information)
- Professional development and continuing education
- B2B referral partner content (CPAs, attorneys)

**YouTube**
- Educational series (e.g., "Retirement Planning 101")
- Explainer videos on complex topics
- Quarterly market reviews
- Financial planning workshops

**Facebook**
- Community engagement for local advisors
- Educational posts
- Office/team culture
- Local event participation

**Email Newsletter** (CRITICAL for Financial Services)
- Monthly market and planning updates
- Deeper dives than social allows
- Calls to schedule annual review
