# Tech Startup Content Template

## Industry: Early-Stage Tech Startups, Product Launch, Founder-Led Brands

For tech startups building in public, generating early users, attracting investors, and establishing thought leadership through founder-led content.

## Template Variables

```json
{
  "STARTUP_NAME": "Your company name",
  "PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION": "What you're building (one clear sentence)",
  "STAGE": "Pre-launch, beta, early traction, growth stage, Series A+",
  "TARGET_USER": "Who uses your product (be specific: 'developers at startups', not 'everyone')",
  "PROBLEM_SOLVING": "Specific pain point you solve (the more niche, the better)",
  "UNIQUE_APPROACH": "What makes your solution different from alternatives",
  "TECH_STACK": "Technologies used (if relevant to audience: 'Built on Next.js + Supabase' for developer tools)",
  "TRACTION_METRICS": "Users, revenue, growth rate (if public)",
  "FOUNDER_BACKGROUND": "Founder expertise and why you're positioned to build this",
  "FUNDING_STATUS": "Bootstrapped, pre-seed, seed, Series A (affects messaging)",
  "MONETIZATION": "Free, freemium, paid tiers, usage-based, enterprise",
  "COMPETITOR_LANDSCAPE": "3-5 direct or adjacent competitors",
  "CONTENT_GOAL": "Waitlist signups, early users, investor attention, talent recruitment, thought leadership"
}
```

## Tech Startup Content Strategy

### Content Pillars

**Build in Public / Founder Journey (40%)**
- Development progress updates
- Lessons learned building the product
- Challenges and how you overcame them
- Metrics and growth transparency
- Behind-the-scenes product decisions
- Founder reflections and insights

**Product Education & Use Cases (30%)**
- Problem-solution fit demonstrations
- Feature announcements and tutorials
- User stories and case studies
- Product demos and walkthroughs
- Integration guides
- Tips and best practices

**Industry Thought Leadership (20%)**
- Trends in your space
- Hot takes and perspectives
- Industry analysis
- Predictions and vision
- "Here's what we're seeing..."
- Technical deep-dives (if appropriate)

**Community Building (10%)**
- User highlights and testimonials
- Community contributions
- Team growth and culture
- Hiring announcements
- Events and meetups
- Changelog and updates

### Platform Strategies

**Twitter/X (PRIMARY for Tech Startups)**
- Daily founder presence and voice
- Build in public updates
- Engage in industry conversations
- Share metrics and traction
- Product updates and launches
- Thought leadership threads

**LinkedIn (For B2B Startups)**
- Founder thought leadership
- Company milestones
- Product launches
- Team growth announcements
- Industry insights
- Fundraising announcements

**Product Hunt (Launch Platform)**
- Coordinate major launches
- Build anticipation pre-launch
- Engage with comments day-of
- Leverage for press and users
- Monthly or quarterly feature releases

**Dev.to / Hacker News (For Developer Tools)**
- Technical deep-dives
- Architecture explanations
- Open source contributions
- Show HN posts
- Technical tutorials

**YouTube / Loom (Product Demos)**
- Product demo videos
- Tutorial content
- Founder video updates
- User testimonials
- Feature walkthroughs

## Content Themes for Startups

**High-Impact Content:**

1. **Build in public metrics** - "Month 6: $8K MRR → $11K (+35%). Here's what worked..."
2. **Founder lessons** - "3 mistakes we made in our first 100 customer calls"
3. **Product decisions explained** - "Why we chose [X] over [Y] for [feature]"
4. **User stories** - "How @company reduced project update time by 90%"
5. **Contrarian takes** - "Why we're NOT building [obvious feature]"
6. **Technical deep-dives** - "How we built real-time sync with 500ms latency"
7. **Transparent challenges** - "We lost our 3rd biggest customer yesterday. Here's why..."
8. **Traction milestones** - "$10K MRR hit! Thread on how we got here 🧵"
9. **Product launches** - "We just shipped [feature]. Here's the 6-month journey..."
10. **Industry insights** - "After 450 customer interviews, here's what we learned about remote eng teams"

### Content Framework: "Founder-Led Transparency"

**70% educational/valuable** - Teach, share lessons, give insights
**20% product/progress** - Updates, features, metrics
**10% promotional** - Explicit asks (signups, hiring, funding)

**Why this works:**
- Builds trust through transparency
- Attracts early adopters who want to support the journey
- Positions founder as expert in problem space
- Creates narrative for investors
- Recruits talent who resonate with mission
