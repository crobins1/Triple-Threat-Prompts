## Content Themes That Win Clients

**High-Converting Content for Agencies:**

1. **"We spent $X to learn this" posts** - Share expensive lessons so prospects see your expertise
2. **Case study carousels** - "How we scaled [Brand] from X to Y" with metrics and screenshots
3. **"Reviewing [Big Brand]'s ads"** - Audit famous brands' marketing to showcase your eye
4. **Contrarian takes** - "Why [common practice] is killing your ROAS" (gets engagement + positions you as expert)
5. **Tool comparisons** - "Triple Whale vs. Northbeam: Which attribution tool for e-com brands?"
6. **Hiring posts** - "We're hiring a media buyer - here's what we look for" (signals growth + attracts clients)
7. **Founder personal story** - "How I went from freelancer to agency" (builds connection)
8. **Client testimonial videos** - 60-90 second video from happy client discussing results
9. **Campaign breakdowns** - "How we generated 4.8 ROAS for [Client]" with strategy + screenshots
10. **Free resources** - "Our 15-point creative testing checklist" (lead magnet → email list → sales)

### Content Framework: The "Expert Who Teaches"

The best agency content positions you as the expert who generously shares knowledge:

- **70% give away the strategy** - "Here's exactly how we do X"
- **30% hint at execution complexity** - "Of course, doing this at scale requires Y, Z, and constant optimization"

This approach:
- Builds trust (you're not gatekeeping)
- Attracts right-fit clients (they understand it's complex)
- Repels wrong-fit clients (DIYers realize it's too much work)
- Generates inbound leads (people want to hire the teacher)

## Platform-Specific Strategy for Agencies

### LinkedIn Strategy (Primary B2B Channel)

**Goal:** Position founders/team as thought leaders, generate inbound leads

**Content Mix:**
- **Monday:** Thought leadership post from founder (marketing trend, contrarian take, lesson learned)
- **Wednesday:** Case study or client win (carousel with metrics)
- **Friday:** Educational how-to or framework (free value)

**Post Format:**
- Hook in first line (ask question or make bold statement)
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines max)
- Use line breaks for readability
- Data/metrics when possible (screenshots build proof)
- Clear CTA ("DM me if you're stuck with X", "Link to full breakdown in comments")

**Engagement Strategy:**
- Comment on industry leaders' posts (get discovered)
- Share client posts (tagging them when they mention you)
- Reply to every comment in first hour

### Instagram Strategy (For Creative Agencies)

**Goal:** Showcase visual work, attract creative-minded clients

**Content Mix:**
- **Feed:** Portfolio pieces, client work, big wins
- **Stories:** Behind-the-scenes, day-in-the-life, team culture
- **Reels:** Quick marketing tips, trend-jacking, ad reviews

**Aesthetic:** Consistent brand colors and fonts, high-quality visuals

### YouTube Strategy (Authority & Education)

**Goal:** Long-form authority building, SEO for agency name + services

**Content Types:**
- **"How we [achieved result] for [client type]"** - 10-15 min case study walkthrough
- **Strategy deep-dives** - "Our complete Meta Ads strategy for e-commerce brands"
- **Tool tutorials** - "How to set up Triple Whale attribution for Shopify"
- **Client interviews** - "Interview with [Client]: How they scaled from X to Y"

**SEO Optimization:**
- Title: "[Result] for [Industry]: [Strategy/Case Study]"
- Description: Include "marketing agency," your specialization, target industry
- Timestamps for sections (improves watch time)

## Unique Challenges for Agency Content

### Challenge 1: Client Confidentiality

**Problem:** Best case studies are often under NDA or client doesn't want to be named

**Solutions:**
- Get client permission in contract: "May we use anonymized results in marketing?"
- Share results without naming client: "E-commerce supplement brand, $2M revenue..."
- Create hypothetical examples: "Here's how we'd approach X for Y type of business"
- Focus on YOUR process, not client details

### Challenge 2: "Why Should We Hire You If You're Teaching This?"

**Positioning:**
- "Strategy is easy to understand, execution is hard to master"
- "You could do this yourself, or we can do it 10x faster with our team and tools"
- "We share this because our ideal clients appreciate transparency"

### Challenge 3: Balancing Self-Promotion vs. Value

**Rule of Thumb:**
- **80% educational value** (tactical how-tos, frameworks, lessons)
- **20% promotional** (case studies, client wins, "we're hiring", sales posts)

### Challenge 4: Standing Out in a Crowded Market

**Differentiation Strategies:**
- **Niche down:** "We only work with Shopify brands doing $1M-10M" (not "e-commerce brands")
- **Unique POV:** "Most agencies optimize for ROAS. We optimize for LTV:CAC." (contrarian)
- **Founder brand:** Personal story, face of agency, not faceless "we"
- **Proprietary methodology:** Name your process ("The Creative-First Framework")
- **Unusual format:** Video essays, long-form Twitter threads, podcast
