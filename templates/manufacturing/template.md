Designed for manufacturing companies, industrial suppliers, and B2B component providers building brand authority and generating qualified leads.

## Manufacturing Content Strategy

### Content Pillars

**Technical Expertise (40%)**
- Manufacturing process explanations
- Material selection guides
- Design for manufacturability tips
- Tolerance and specification education
- Application engineering insights

**Capabilities Showcase (30%)**
- Equipment and technology features
- Project case studies
- Before/after transformations
- Complex part highlights
- Industry-specific solutions

**Thought Leadership (20%)**
- Industry trends and innovations
- Supply chain insights
- Quality control methodologies
- Sustainability practices
- Regulatory compliance updates

**Company Credibility (10%)**
- Certifications and awards
- Customer testimonials
- Facility tours
- Team expertise
- Community involvement

**LinkedIn (PRIMARY for B2B Manufacturing)**
- Technical thought leadership
- Case studies and applications
- Industry trend commentary
- New capabilities announcements
- Customer success stories

**YouTube**
- Facility and equipment tours
- Manufacturing process videos
- "How it's made" content
- Quality inspection processes

**Website/Blog (SEO Critical)**
- Technical resource library

- Industry-specific solutions
- Capability statements

**Trade Shows & Industry Events**
- Live demonstrations
- Video content from booth
- Speaker presentations
